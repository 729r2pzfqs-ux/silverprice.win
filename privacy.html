Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

SilverPrice.win ("we", "us") is a free website that displays live precious-metal prices. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the site.

Information We Collect

We do not require accounts, registration, or any personal details. We do not collect names, email addresses, or payment information.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site usage (pages visited, approximate location by country/city, device and browser type). Google Analytics may set cookies and collect your IP address and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Local Storage

The site saves your preferences (selected currency, language, and light/dark theme) in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

To display live data, your browser makes requests directly to third-party services. These providers receive your IP address as part of the normal operation of the web:

  • Kitco — live spot prices for gold, silver, platinum, and palladium
  • TradingView — embedded interactive price charts (TradingView may set its own cookies inside the chart frame; see TradingView's Privacy Policy)
  • ExchangeRate-API — currency conversion rates
  • Cloudflare Workers — regional price benchmarks (Shanghai, India MCX)
  • Google Fonts — font files

Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves. Cookies may be set by Google Analytics and by embedded third-party content (TradingView charts) as described above.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any data to third parties beyond the services listed above, which are necessary to operate the site.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
